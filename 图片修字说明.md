# 图片修字与提示词

使用内置 imagegen 编辑文字，然后只合成对应文字遮罩的像素。原版整图不进入补丁；游戏在中文语言下从原图重建修字结果。
所有最终修字差量保存于 game/zhcn_image_deltas.json，由 game/zz_zhcn_images.rpy 载入。

## 最终提示词组

共同约束：Use case: text-localization. Change only the specified lettering. Preserve the original aspect ratio, framing, positions, palette, blur, grain, chromatic aberration, transparency, and all non-text subjects. Do not sharpen, enhance, or redraw the scene.

- 镜子迷宫：Replace MIRROR MAZE with 镜子迷宫 along the same arch. Match the muted pink lettering and red/green fringes. Keep entrance and lights unchanged.
- 电影院门面：Replace CINEMA with 电影院. Match soft pink letters, perspective, blur and grain.
- 售票大厅：Replace the left CINEMA with 影院 and cropped TICKETS with 售票. Keep both signs partially cropped as originally framed.
- 手机故障帧 07c、07d：Replace every come back with 回来. Keep individual bubble geometry, circle avatars and the existing RGB ghost offsets. Include clipped text at image edges.
- 手机故障帧 07e：Replace come back in both large dark teal and small pink bubbles with 回来. Retain original scan lines and noise; do not rebuild the message column.
- 错误帧 07a、07b、07c、08a：Replace ERROR/eRRoR with 错误 at the existing size levels. Preserve jagged pixel outlines, red/yellow fills, RGB offsets and the street backdrop.
- 困难迷宫结局图标：Replace AWESOME GAMER inside the yellow medal with 游戏高手, two lines 游戏 then 高手. Keep rough orange handwriting, blue trim, ribbon and cat.
- 手机结局图标：Replace NEW MESSAGE with 新消息 in matching rough black block lettering. Keep phone, pink screen and border.

追逐、黑白、淡出帧沿用各自原图，只在同一文字范围匹配对应的原有色调。睡眠 Z 字符号、时钟数字和密码字母保留；未被剧情引用的 phoneTest 测试图不纳入补丁。

逐图范围检查见“图片文字范围检查.json”。该检查证明遮罩外和透明度没有改变；擦除旧字所需的字间背景、描边与色偏边缘属于修字遮罩范围。
